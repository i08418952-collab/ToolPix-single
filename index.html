How to use ToolPix

Edit or convert an image or PDF in three simple steps.

  1. Choose a tool

    Pick the tool you need, such as JPG to PNG, Resize Image or Compress Image.

  2. Add your file

    Select or drag in your image or PDF. It opens in your browser and is not uploaded.

  3. Download the result

    Adjust the settings if you want, then save the finished file to your device.

Frequently asked questions

Are ToolPix tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on ToolPix is free to use, and no account or sign-up is required.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your images and PDF files are processed directly in your browser, so they stay on your device.

Which file formats can I use?

You can work with JPG, PNG and WebP images and PDF files. ToolPix converts JPG to PNG, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG and between JPG, PNG and WebP.

Does ToolPix work on my phone?

Yes. ToolPix works in modern browsers on phones, tablets and computers.

How does the background remover work?

The Remove Background tool works best on simple, plain backgrounds and saves the result as a transparent PNG.

How can I make an image file smaller?

Use Compress Image to reduce the file size, or Resize Image to lower the width and height. Both run in your browser.
